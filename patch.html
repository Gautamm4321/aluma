WHAT IT IS

Real hair, bonded to a second skin.

A hair patch is a custom-made base of ultra-thin, breathable skin material, hand-knotted with human hair matched to your natural colour, texture and density.

It's measured to your scalp, cut to follow your own hairline, and bonded with a skin-friendly adhesive. Once fitted, it washes, dries and combs exactly like your own hair.

The base is chosen for your scalp sensitivity and lifestyle — thin skin bases give the most natural, undetectable finish, while mesh bases offer extra breathability for Bengaluru's humidity.

Hair is 100% remy human hair, hand-knotted strand by strand so it falls exactly like natural growth. Density, direction and grey percentage are customised to you.

Unlike a transplant, there's no surgery and no months-long wait — you leave with a complete head of hair, and the look can be redesigned later if you want a change.

THE ALUMA METHOD

Your makeover, mapped to the hour.

01 ~15 min

Consultation & scalp mapping

We assess your hair loss pattern and scalp condition, then match a base and hair type to your colour, texture and grain.

02 ~30 min

Custom fit, bond & blend

The patch is trimmed to your hairline and bonded with a skin-friendly adhesive, then blended with your existing hair.

03 ~15 min

Cut & style

Our stylist cuts and styles the new hair into your chosen look. Same hour, complete transformation.

First few days: slight tightness at the bonded edge is normal and settles within 3–4 days — it isn't painful.

Daily care: wash with a sulphate-free shampoo 2–3 times a week, pat dry, brush gently from the ends upward.

Touch-ups: return every 3–5 weeks for rebonding. A single unit typically lasts 6–12 months before replacement.

Lifestyle: workouts, swimming and Bengaluru's humidity are all fine — the bond holds through sweat and daily activity.

PATCH VS. TRANSPLANT

Why most clients choose non-surgical first.

Aluma

Hair Patch

  • Results visible the same day
  • No cuts, needles or anaesthesia
  • No recovery time
  • Fully reversible, restyle anytime
  • Touch-up every 3–5 weeks; lasts 6–12 months
  • Significantly lower upfront cost

Surgical Transplant

  • Results take 6–12 months to mature
  • Follicle extraction & implantation surgery
  • Days of downtime
  • Permanent — cannot be redesigned
  • Limited by available donor hair
  • Higher upfront cost

A transplant relocates your own follicles from the back or sides of the scalp — a genuine medical procedure with real recovery time, and results depend on available donor hair.

A hair patch doesn't touch your biology — it's a precision-fitted system, the better starting point if you want an immediate result or prefer a non-invasive option long-term.

Many clients use both across their journey: a patch for immediate confidence now, with surgery an option to explore later.
