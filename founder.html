A Note From Our Founders

Three Minds. One Vision. A Shared Dream.

ALUMA was born from the shared ambition of Sudhanshu Kumar, Somesh Anand, and Satyam Sundaram to rethink the way people experience salons.

United by a common vision, we set out to bring together personal care, technology, and convenience to create something more meaningful than a traditional salon brand.

We believe a great salon experience is not just about looking good — it's about feeling confident, being valued, and walking out with a smile.

As founders, we're building ALUMA with passion, a willingness to learn, and the courage to challenge the ordinary. Every step of our journey brings us closer to our dream of making quality grooming more accessible and the salon experience better for everyone.

We're not just building a brand. We're building a vision we believe in.

— Sudhanshu Kumar, Somesh Anand & Satyam Sundaram
